How Round Call works

Every Squeekchain round is 10 minutes. During a round you call what Squeektoshi does in the next one; the real readings settle it when that round ends. Nap and jog pay 1 point, zoomies 3, and a streak is yours to keep.

Your score stays in this browser. Streamers get the same game on their overlay, with Twitch chat calling in: see the overlays.
