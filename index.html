Round Call, goals, frame and scenes

Round Call is the game: during each 10-minute Squeekchain round your viewers call what Squeektoshi does in the next one. With a Twitch channel the overlay tallies the chat's calls, shows the result and keeps a leaderboard for the stream; without one, viewers play on the second screen and the watch page.

Add it in OBS or Streamlabs

  1. Copy a link above.
  2. In OBS: Sources, +, Browser. In Streamlabs: Add Source, Browser Source. Paste the link as the URL.
  3. Set the width and height shown next to the link. Leave the custom CSS empty: the pages are already transparent (the scenes are full dark screens on purpose).
  4. For sounds tick "Control audio via OBS", and untick "Shutdown source when not visible".

What sets off an alert

AlertWhen
Wake upThe wheel turns again on two readings in a row after at least three minutes still.
NapThree minutes without a turn after he was running.
ZoomiesRunning at or above your threshold (4.5 km/h by default) on two readings in a row. Once per burst.
Block foundA settlement on SqueekCore completes one or more Squeek blocks.
Top speedSqueekchain records a new top speed.
DistanceLifetime and daily distance milestones.
StreakDays in a row over 5 km.
MergeSomeone merges two Squeeks (off by default).
Goal reachedThe stream goal bar fills up (on the goal overlay itself).

Readings come from Squeekchain's public API every 5 seconds, the event feed every 15. Reloading a source never replays old alerts. If the API is down the board shows OFF AIR and keeps the last values.
